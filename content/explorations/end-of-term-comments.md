At the end of each trimester a teacher writes a short comment for every learner, often for more than a hundred of them. The data to say something precise is there (scores, progress, activity), but gathering it takes time, so comments tend to be generic.

This is the teachers' side of the same data as the [chatbot](/explorations/mentions) and the [grading workflow](/explorations/konsolidation-grading): the AI proposes, the teacher decides.

## Questions to explore

- **What goes in the draft?** Only facts from the data, or also an interpretation. The second is more useful and easier to get wrong.
- **How do you keep the teacher's voice?** A hundred comments from one model sound alike, and learners and parents notice.
- **How much editing is too much?** If every draft needs a rewrite, it saves nothing. If none does, nobody is checking.
- **What about privacy?** These comments are about minors, so what the model reads matters, as it does for [mentions](/explorations/mentions).
